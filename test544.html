Common questions

What companies usually ask before starting.

What is the difference between AI Support and Managed CX?

AI Support gives you a continuously trained AI assistant while your team owns any human escalations. Managed CX adds NovaraDesk, a dedicated human agent, ticket ownership and CX quality management.

Can Novara support both B2B and B2C companies?

Yes. The operating model works wherever there are repeatable customer questions plus occasional cases that require human judgement. The knowledge, workflows and tone are configured for your business.

Does the AI improve after launch?

Yes. Continuous training and tuning are core parts of both plans. We review conversations, update knowledge and improve response quality rather than leaving the bot unmanaged.

What happens when the AI cannot answer?

The conversation is marked for human escalation with its context preserved. In Managed CX, Novara’s human support layer takes ownership directly.

Which channels can be connected?

The MVP supports website chat, with additional channels and integrations confirmed based on your existing support stack during discovery.
