The hidden cost of broken CX

When CX is broken, it's not just tickets. It's a cascade of risks that hit your brand where it hurts most.

Escalations

Founders spend 20+ hours a week firefighting escalations that shouldn't reach them. Every hour spent on a refund is an hour not spent on strategy.

Social Media Damage

One angry customer goes viral. One screenshot on Twitter. One negative review on Amazon. Your brand reputation takes a hit that takes months to repair.

Legal Risks

Missed SLAs, broken promises, misleading communication — all can lead to consumer complaints, regulatory scrutiny, and legal exposure.

Founder Time Drain

When you're in the weeds of customer support, you're not building product, fundraising, or scaling. CX becomes a distraction, not a growth engine.
