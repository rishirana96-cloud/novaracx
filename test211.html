05 FAQs

Before we talk.

Can we keep our existing support team?

Yes. With Managed AI Support, Novara maintains the AI and your team continues to own the conversations that need a person.

What can we use to train the AI?

Start with your help articles, policies, FAQs, and the answers your team already gives. During setup, we identify where your knowledge needs an update or a clear escalation rule.

Which channels can we start with?

Website chat and WhatsApp Business are starting points. We’ll discuss your setup and the channels you need on the discovery call.

Can we set our own support hours?

We agree human coverage hours with you based on the plan and your needs. AI can handle configured questions outside those hours.
